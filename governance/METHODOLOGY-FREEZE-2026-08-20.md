# Procedure freeze until the AXIS3 verdict — 2026-08-20

The m3-deps quarantine verdict is computed per the record of 2026-08-18
(AXIS3-DEPS-V2H2-SUPERSESSION: three forward partitions; verdict window
**2026-09-14**). From this date until the verdict is recorded, no methodology
changes will be made.

**Why:** the axis-3 verdict must be computed under a procedure fixed before its
outcome is known, and launch-period criticism must not be able to reshape a
procedure mid-evaluation.

**Frozen:** axis definitions and thresholds · the admission predicate · gate rules ·
measurement-state definitions · the activation/tranche rule (as published in
REGISTRY-GROWTH-POLICY-2026-07-21).

**Not frozen:** correctness defects and data errors — fixed immediately via dated
errata that do not alter the predicate; display typography. Any change to strings
that appear next to a system name is a dated public record
(see LEXICON-SEMANTICS-2026-08-20), never a footnote.

**Slip protocol:** if the verdict slips past 2026-09-14, the freeze extends with it
and the slip is recorded.

**Feedback channels:** data discrepancies and bugs → immediate dated errata;
methodology proposals → dated public queue, considered only for the next versioned
record after the verdict.

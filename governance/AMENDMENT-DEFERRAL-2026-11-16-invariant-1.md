## 1. What is decided here

The decision on the amendment of invariant 1 is not taken on the earliest valid date,
2026-11-18. It is taken on the day the switch record is published, in the same week as
the person-free switch, and not later than the control date in section 4.

## 2. Why

- Section 8, step 3 of the proposal says the decision names "the switch record it relies
  on". Section 4 says the dates in the texts "are fixed by the switch record and the
  decision". The switch record does not exist yet, so a decision signed now would be
  incomplete under the proposal's own procedure.
- The target switch, the weekly release of 2026-11-21 (section 7.3), will not happen. The
  implementation has not started. A new date is fixed by the switch record.
- New texts that describe a pre-publication check would be false while that check is not
  running. Changing the texts together with the switch keeps the constitution true.
- This would be the first signature with the key of `governance/KEY-REGISTRY-2026-10-19.md`.
  It is made on a complete decision.

The earliest date of section 8 is a lower bound. The Amendment rule of `CONSTITUTION.md`
sets no upper bound.

## 3. What does not change

- The switch is required by the current text of invariant 1 and `RIGHTS-BASIS.md`,
  whatever the decision (proposal, sections 7.3 and 7.5). Deferring the decision does not
  defer the switch.
- Until the switch, the project stays out of line with the publication rule, as stated
  in section 7.1 of the proposal. This record does not describe that state as compliance.
- The erasure procedure (`governance/ERASURE-REQUESTS-2026-10-19.md`) stays in force.
- The comment channel of section 9 stays open until the decision.

## 4. Control date

If no switch record is published by **2026-12-31**, a dated record on that date states
the state of the implementation and one of two courses: a new switch date, or a decision
on the texts alone with the gap stated.

The falsification window of section 10 ends on 2026-12-31 and assumes an earlier switch.
The decision counts it from the switch and states its new end.
